SEEDS: 1:100, identical across all five arms (paired). Seed = the only
resampling unit; every mean is reported with sd and jackknife SEM
(l0_jackknife_se.m). Paired differences (OFF - ON) per seed, t = mean/SEM.

ARMS (opts.ctrl_const_override):
  A0 base      : none
  A1 echo_off  : y2_echo_corr = false
  A2 ma2_off   : ma2_aug = false
  A3 fe4_off   : fe_row4_full = false
  A4 both_off  : y2_echo_corr = false AND ma2_aug = false

WINDOWS (driver's own): descent [0.5, 1.5] s; oscillation (1.6, 3.5] s;
final hold [3.8, 4.8] s. Axis z (column 3). Init row dropped by the L0 loader.
Trough truth a_T = mean normalised a_true over the final hold, per seed.
Primary a_hat effect = final-hold z bias, per seed:
  bias_pct = 100 * mean_hold(a_hat - a_true) / a_T           [driver hold_mean_pct
  up to the mean-of-ratio vs ratio-of-mean distinction; both reported].
"a_hat movement" thresholds are on |paired diff of bias_pct| in percentage
points of a_T, and on its t-stat. 1 SEM of the baseline bias_pct across
seeds is the reference unit for the stop rule.

SIGN RULE: every threshold below is on the ABSOLUTE value of the paired
difference or of the OFF-arm statistic, so a sign change counts as a change.
Signed values are reported alongside.

(a) y2_echo_corr  (S_T/S_n from the Lyapunov form at init, lambda_c 0.7)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original conviction (formB shallow, 08-01): w_s honesty 1.42 -> 0.93.
Not available on formC (no w_s state). Registered formC signature:
  Q_a1 = mean of innov_y2 (z) over the final hold, gate-open samples only,
         per seed. Paired diff OFF - ON, t-stat over 100 seeds.
  Q_a2 = Var(innov_y2 hold) / mean(R2 hold), per seed, both arms (the 08-19
         result: this ratio ~1 and does NOT need (1-S)^2).
  Q_a3 = |K_a_y2| mean over the hold, both arms (mechanical check: H2 scales by
         1/(1-S) ~ 1.47 when OFF, so K_a_y2 must move; if it does not, the flag
         is not wired -- rule B6).
Expectations:
  REPRODUCED : |t(Q_a1 diff)| >= 3  AND  Q_a2 within [0.8, 1.25] on both arms
               AND |a_hat effect| < 2.0 pp of a_T.
  EXPIRED    : |t(Q_a1 diff)| < 2  (no systematic innov2 offset appears when
               the correction is removed), whatever a_hat does.
  OTHER      : 2 <= |t| < 3, or Q_a2 leaves [0.8, 1.25], or |a_hat effect|
               >= 2.0 pp (mechanism present but not the registered one).
Expected a_hat movement: < 2.0 pp of a_T (y2 carries ~1% of the gain update).
Note (declared before running): in the final hold Grad_wbar_d = 0, so the
y2 PREDICTION is identical on both arms there; the arms differ in H2 (gain
scale) only. The registered offset therefore has to arrive through the
filter's equilibrium (a_hat, P), not through the prediction line. This is
recorded so a null result cannot later be reinterpreted as "wrong window".

(c) fe_row4_full  (row-4 multiplier M_row4 = Delta_wbar_d[k-1] + (1-lc) dw3_hat)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original conviction (08-01 comment, no stored number): dropping (1-lc)*dw3_hat
zeroes the parameter columns F_e(4,5) (and (4,3)) at every trajectory turning
point (command increment = 0). Only the Jacobian is affected: x_pred(4) at
controller :1186 uses the full bracket on both arms, so the state propagation
is unchanged and the OFF arm differs only through P.
Direct quantity M_tot is not logged; the registered PROXY is the b-channel
Kalman gain, which is fed by P(5,4) that F_e(4,5) builds:
  Q_c1 = mean |K_b_y1| (z) in a +-10-step window around each turning point of
         h_bar_d inside [1.5, 3.5] s (local extrema of the commanded height,
         top and trough, both included; sub-split reported), per seed, both
         arms. Ratio OFF/ON per seed and paired diff.
  Q_c2 = same for |K_b_y2|.
  Q_c3 = same |K_b_y1| over the FULL oscillation window (control: if the
         whole-window ratio is as small as the turning-point ratio, the effect
         is not turning-point specific).
  Q_c4 = a_hat effect (bias_pct paired diff), t-stat; also b_hat(end) diff.
Expectations:
  REPRODUCED : median over seeds of ratio Q_c1(OFF)/Q_c1(ON) <= 0.5 at the
               turning points AND that ratio is smaller (by >= 0.2) than the
               full-window ratio Q_c3(OFF)/Q_c3(ON). (Turning-point specific
               attenuation of the b-channel gain.)
  EXPIRED    : turning-point ratio within [0.8, 1.25] (|K_b| unchanged when
               the term is dropped).
  OTHER      : anything else (e.g. |K_b| GROWS when OFF, or attenuation that is
               not turning-point specific).
Caveat registered: with ma2_aug still ON on this arm, M_tot keeps the
alpha*(m1+m2) share, so F_e(4,5) does not go EXACTLY to zero at turning
points -- an attenuation, not a null, is what the proxy can show. And K_b is
an accumulated covariance quantity, so the proxy lags the instantaneous
column by the P dynamics; the +-10-step window (6.25 ms at 1600 Hz) is where
|Delta_wbar_d| < 4% of its peak.
Expected a_hat movement: unknown; reported.

Counts per seed (z axis and also x/y) of samples with

else the controller defaults quoted above.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STOP RULE (report only; nothing is removed by this battery)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A member is a REMOVAL CANDIDATE when its signature is EXPIRED and
|a_hat effect| < 1 SEM of the baseline bias_pct (i.e. |t of the paired
diff| < 1 -- equivalent, since SEM(diff) is what t uses; both numbers are
printed). A member whose signature is REPRODUCED is retained regardless of
a_hat effect. OTHER verdicts are reported as such and go back to the ledger
as open.

Deviations from this registration will be listed explicitly in the report.
