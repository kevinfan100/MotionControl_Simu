\section*{What this writing is, and what it costs}

The gain slope is read off the GAIN STATE rather than off a height:
$\ba' = b(1-\ba)^{2}$ evaluated at $\hat{\ba}_w$. That is the whole difference
from the height writing, and everything below follows from it.

The price is structural, not a tuning failure. Because $\ba'$ depends on
$\hat{\ba}_w$, the error recursion gains the term $-2\hat{b}(1-\hat{\ba}_w)M$,
so the homogeneous coefficient exceeds 1 while the stage descends. Over the
canonical descent that factor accumulates to $220\times$, and that number is not
an artefact: the true slope at the trough is $107\times$ the slope at the seed,
so the writing converts a real property of the physics into error growth. The
height writing's coefficient for that path is exactly zero.

The filter prices it correctly and can do nothing else. $F_e(4,4)$ carries the
term, so $P_{44}$ grows $1.8\times$ during motion, $L$ grows $5.6\times$ on
$y_1$ and $23$--$39\times$ on $y_2$, and the estimate leans on measurements that
are unbiased and noisy. The across-seed spread is $6.4\times$ the exogenous
arm's. That is the bias-for-scatter trade, bought deliberately by an optimal
filter facing a model it should not trust.

Demonstrated with a deterministic injection: seeding $b$ at 1 instead of $8/9$
puts $\hat{\ba}_w[0]$ a known $+0.0056$ above the truth, identical across all
eight seeds and carrying no random information. Fed $\ba'$ from the true height
the offset is erased and the across-seed band stays narrow; fed $\ba'$ from the
belief it is not erased and the band opens out. A common-mode model error
becomes seed-dependent scatter, and the split appears only once the stage moves.
Figure \texttt{formC\_belief\_vs\_command.png}.

\section*{What the reparameterisation bought}

$b$ multiplies rather than divides (user decision, 2026-08-18). Same curve
family, $b_{\mathrm{new}} = 1/b_{\mathrm{old}}$, so the far-field limit inverts
$9/8 \to 8/9$ while contact stays exactly 1.

Every known truncation is unchanged in magnitude, for one structural reason:
each is a statement about the product $\ba' = b(1-\ba)^{2}$, and that product is
the reparameterisation invariant. Even the relative prior width is exactly
invariant, since $\mathrm{half}/\mathrm{mid} = (hi-lo)/(hi+lo)$ is fixed under
$x \mapsto 1/x$: $1.21052\,\%$ either way.

One thing is strictly better and had not been written down. In the old form
$\ba' = (1-\ba)^{2}/b$ is nonlinear in $b$, so
$\partial^{2}\ba'/\partial b^{2} = 2(1-\ba)^{2}/b^{3}$ and a curvature term was
dropped from $F_e(4,5)$ and $H(2,5)$ every step, worth $e_b/\hat{b} = 1.21\,\%$
at the one-sigma prior. In the new form $\ba'$ is exactly linear in $b$ and that
term is identically zero: the $b$ column is now exact.

\section*{S1 --- the truth, and what the model replaces}

$\ba_{\mathrm{true}}$ is $D_{\perp}$ from \texttt{calc\_correction\_functions}
character for character, since $\ba := a/a_o = 1/c_{\perp}$ and
$c_{\perp} = 1/D_{\perp}$.

Because $\ba_{\mathrm{true}}$ is monotone in $\bw$, $\bw$ can be eliminated
EXACTLY and the autonomous law $\ba' = b_{\mathrm{true}}(\ba)(1-\ba)^{2}$ carries
no approximation. The model's single approximation is replacing the function
$b_{\mathrm{true}}(\ba)$ by a constant. Stating it this way also dissolves the
apparent conflict between the two anchors: $b_{\mathrm{true}}$ is a curve, its
ends simply read differently, and choosing an end is a design decision.

Verified at 30 digits: $\ba_{\mathrm{true}}(1) = 0$ to $5\times10^{-32}$,
$\ba'_{\mathrm{true}}(1) = 1$ exactly, so $b_{\mathrm{true}}(\ba = 0) = 1$; and
$b_{\mathrm{true}} \to 8/9$ FROM BELOW.

$b_{\mathrm{true}}$ is NOT monotone. It rises from an interior minimum
$0.866978$ at $\bw = 2.193$ to $0.888225$ at the envelope top and never reaches
$8/9$. The envelope range is therefore $[0.866978,\ 0.888225]$, whose lower end
is an interior extremum rather than an endpoint.

\section*{Seed and prior --- the seed and the width come from different things}

\begin{equation*}
\hat{b}[0] = \tfrac{8}{9},
\qquad
\sqrt{P_{55}[0]} = \sup_{\bw\in\mathcal{E}}
  \bigl|b_{\mathrm{true}}(\bw) - \tfrac{8}{9}\bigr| = 0.021911
\end{equation*}

THE SEED is the analytic anchor because $P[0]$ is the belief at $t=0$ and the
run starts in the far field. At the seed height $\bw = 22.22$ the truth is
$b_{\mathrm{true}} = 0.888167$; the anchor misses it by $7.2\times10^{-4}$, the
envelope midpoint $0.877602$ by $1.06\times10^{-2}$ --- fifteen times worse,
because the midpoint is dragged down by an interior minimum the run reaches
twenty radii later. Measured over the eight house seeds, seeding at the midpoint
costs $+1.81$ points of overall RMS, sd $1.52$, SEM $0.54$, $t = +3.38$, worse
on $8/8$. This is the same defect as sizing $\mathrm{floor}_a$ from the envelope
supremum, which cost $2.9$ points and was repaired the same day: both take a
statistic over ground the run has not covered and use it to set a belief at
$t=0$.

THE WIDTH is a statement about the run, not about $t=0$. $b$ is a constant state
($b[k+1] = b[k]$, $Q_{55} = 0$), so one number must serve the whole traverse
including the near wall. That is step 4 of the $P[0]$ chain in
\texttt{reference/shared/param\_prior\_rules.md}.

KNOWN CONSEQUENCE, recorded rather than repaired. The shape-acceptance criterion
compares $\sup|\theta_{\mathrm{eff}} - \theta_0|$ against $\sqrt{P[0]}$, and with
$\theta_0 = 8/9$ those are the same supremum, so the ratio is identically
$1.0000$ and the test cannot fail. That is a defect in the TEST's denominator.
Distorting the prior to give the test something to do is what mis-seeded the
filter, so it is not done. Related: the tool the criterion names,
\texttt{verify\_shape\_exponent\_bound.m}, DOES NOT EXIST in this repository, so
no member of this family has been through it. That is the missing acceptance
artifact.

CHARTER TENSION. $\mathcal{E}$ is built from \texttt{cfg.h\_bottom} and
\texttt{cfg.h\_init}, so $\sqrt{P_{55}[0]}$ is trajectory-dependent, while
CLAUDE.md describes this family's prior as global and trajectory-independent.
The anchor form of the seed is global; the width is not.

CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER READING. $b[k+1] = b[k]$ with $Q_{55} = 0$ alongside
S1's varying $b_{\mathrm{true}}$ is NOT a self-contradiction. It is the six-step
$P[0]$ chain executed correctly, with the prior serving as the container for the
variation. What was genuinely missing was this citation.

\section*{Truncations written with ``='' --- seven, none previously declared}

Magnitudes are on the production scenario, $R = 2.25\,\mu$m, $\Delta t = 1/1600$,

$\mathcal{E} = [1.9,\ 23.222]$.

\begin{enumerate}
\item S3's $\ba_w[k+1] = \ba_w + \ba'_{\mathrm{true}}\Sigma$ is forward Euler for
$\ba(\bw+\Sigma)$. Dropped: $\tfrac12\ba''\Sigma^{2}$. Per-step relative error
$b(1-\ba)|\Sigma| = 0.200\,\%$ at the trough, $0.133\,\%$ over the cycle;
accumulated $+5.90\times10^{-4}$ per oscillation cycle. $\ba'' < 0$ everywhere
and the term goes as $\Sigma^{2}$, so it does NOT change sign with direction of
travel: a closed oscillation does not cancel it and the estimator runs high.
This is the project's quadrature ratchet.

expansion also drops the cross term $-2(1-\hat{\ba}_w)e_{\ba}e_b M$, about
$1.25\,\%$ of the retained $e_b$ column.

\item S7's back-off uses the COMMANDED $\nabla_d\bw_d$; the actual $d$-step

\item The same back-off is itself forward Euler over a $d=2$ gap, carrying four

\item S8's $q_3 = -\ba_w\bar{f}_T$ dropped the MA(2) memory and the

\section*{An assumption violation the truncations hide}

$\varepsilon_w$ contains $-(1-\lambda_c)n_w[k]$ (sign corrected 2026-08-30 from
the control law: the delayed measurement enters the tracking error through
$-(1-\lambda_c)(\bw - \hat{w})$ and $\bw - \hat{w}$ carries $-n_w$; the code
header had the minus already) and $y_1$'s noise IS $n_w[k]$, so
$E[\mathbf{q}\mathbf{v}^{\top}] \neq 0$, which the standard Kalman filter this
document invokes forbids, and $R = \mathrm{diag}(R_1,R_2)$ carries no cross
term. Coefficient $0.0157$. Small, but structural rather than approximate --- and
invisible inside S8's own truncated $q_3$, which drops the $n_w$ share. The
truncation hides the violation, which is why it survived.

\section*{Normalization scales (2026-08-30)}

Three scales, not two. Lengths by $R$ and the gain by
$a_{\mathrm{nom}} = \Delta t/\gamma_N$ $[\mu\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{pN}]$ leave
$\Delta\bw = (a_{\mathrm{nom}}/R)\,\ba\,f$ with a dimensional coefficient
$a_{\mathrm{nom}}/R =: a_o$ $[1/\mathrm{pN}]$ still in the state equation: the
unit of the gain contains $1/\mathrm{pN}$, which belongs to the force, and only
the force can absorb it. The third scale is therefore a force,
$f_R = 1/a_o = \gamma_N R/\Delta t$ (the force that moves the probe one radius
in one step at far field; $153$ pN on the house constants), and
$\bar{f} = f/f_R = a_o f$ makes $\Delta\bw = \ba\bar{f}$ with coefficient one.

This is why $Q_{33} = 4k_BT\,a_o\ba/R$ carries $R$ and not $R^{2}$: $a_o$ is
the $R$-normalized far-field gain, already holding one $1/R$. Written with the
physical gain the same quantity is $4k_BT\,a_{\mathrm{nom}}\ba/R^{2}$. The two
forms are numerically identical; the question ``$R$ or $R^{2}$?'' (asked in the
lab, 2026-08-30) is a question about which $a_o$ is meant, and the notation line
of the main file now says which. $\xi = C_n\sigma^{2}_{n_w}/(C_{dpmr}\kappa_T)$
is in units of $\ba$ ($C_{dpmr}$, $C_n$ are pure functions of
$\lambda_c$, $a_{pd}$; $\sigma^{2}_{n_w}$ and $\kappa_T$ are both in $R^{2}$),
which is why it can be added to $\ba$.

The controller crosses the unit boundary at five places and nowhere else:
U0 init (\texttt{:355--360}: $\sigma^{2}_n/R^{2}$, $a_o$, $\kappa_T$,
$a_{\mathrm{disp}} = a_oR = a_{\mathrm{nom}}$); U1 every step
(\texttt{:996, :1013--1016, :1050}: lengths $/R$); U1$'$
(\texttt{:981}: an override gain in $\mu$m/pN, $/a_{\mathrm{disp}}$); U3
(\texttt{:1075}: $f_d = \bar{f}_d/a_o$, the only de-normalization that reaches
the plant); U4 display (\texttt{:922--937, :1517--1591}: $\times a_{\mathrm{disp}}$,
$\times R$; a legacy $\times a_o$ family gives $1/$pN and must not be mixed
with $a_{\mathrm{disp}}$). The plant knows only $\gamma_N$, $c(\bw)$ and
$\Delta t$. Changing the definition of $a_o$ therefore means changing the power
of $R$ in $\kappa_T$ and the division at U3 in the same edit; changing
\texttt{:357} alone runs to completion with every number wrong by $R = 2.25$.

Figure axes follow the physical-gain convention: a normalized gain axis is
labelled $a_z/a_{\mathrm{nom}}$ (or $\ba_z$), never $a_z/a_o$, which is a
length. Hats sit on normalized quantities only.

three arms use the seed-local $\mathrm{floor}_a$ and the anchored $b$ prior.

free $b$ is a locked $b$ plus a noise source. The ceiling with a perfect
integrand is $1.16\,\%$ against the $2.20\,\%$ achieved, and that gap is the
price of reading $\ba'$ at the belief.

sample alignment corrected the bias is zero in every phase --- $-0.74$ nm on the
descent, $-1.04 \pm 3.29$ nm over the eight seeds in the initial hold, $t=-0.89$
