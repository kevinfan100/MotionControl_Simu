\section{Gain law}

(bars denote normalized quantities: lengths by $R$, $\ba = a/a_o$,
$\bar{f} = a_o f$; $0 < \ba < 1$; $e_{\theta} = \theta - \hat{\theta}$)

The slope is read off the gain state alone. No parameter appears in the law.

\begin{equation*}
\ba'(\ba) := \frac{\mathrm{d}\ba}{\mathrm{d}\bw} = \bigl(1-\ba\bigr)^{2}
\end{equation*}

\begin{equation*}
a_w = a_o\,\ba_w , \qquad a_o = \frac{\Delta t}{\gamma_N R}
\qquad (a_o \text{ fixed, not a state})
\end{equation*}

Integrated form (used for seeding only; $\bw_0$ is the integration constant,
i.e.\ setting $\hat{\ba}_w[0]$ IS setting the wall position)

\begin{equation*}
\ba(\bw) = 1 - \frac{1}{\bw - \bw_0} ,
\qquad
\frac{\partial \ba}{\partial \bw_0} = -\,\ba' \quad(\text{exact})
\end{equation*}

Declared truncation. The truth is not a member of this one-curve family. With
$b_{\mathrm{state}}(\bw) := (1-\ba_{\mathrm{true}})^{2}/\ba'_{\mathrm{true}}$,
measured offline on $\bw-\bw_s \in [0.1,\,25]$:

\begin{equation*}
\frac{\ba'(\ba_{\mathrm{true}})}{\ba'_{\mathrm{true}}} - 1
  = b_{\mathrm{state}}(\bw) - 1
\qquad
\begin{array}{l}
13.05\,\%\ \mathrm{RMS},\quad 15.34\,\%\ \sup \\[0.2em]
\text{range } [+7.79,\ +15.34]\,\%,\ \textbf{one-signed} \\[0.2em]
\text{far field } \to +1/8 \text{ exactly } (b_{\mathrm{state}} \to 9/8)
\end{array}
\end{equation*}

This error is what derivation (b) asks the disturbance to absorb. Derivation
(a) carries it with no container at all.

\section{True dynamics}

$\bigl(\delta\bw_j[k] := \delta\bw[k-d+j-1],\ j = 1,\dots,d+1,\ d = 2\bigr)$

\begin{align*}
\bw[k] &= \bw_d[k] - \delta\bw_3[k] \\
\bw_d[k+1] &= \bw_d[k] + \Delta\bw_d[k] \\
\delta\bw_1[k+1] &= \delta\bw_2[k] \\
\delta\bw_2[k+1] &= \delta\bw_3[k] \\

\begin{align*}

  + (1-\lambda_c)\sum_{i=1}^{d}\ba_w[k-i]\,\bar{f}_T[k-i]

\end{align*}

The gain follows the TRUE slope, which is not the law:

\begin{equation*}
\ba_w[k+1] = \ba_w[k] + \ba'_{\mathrm{true}}[k]\,\Bigl[
  \Delta\bw_d[k] + (1-\lambda_c)\,\delta\bw_3[k]

\end{equation*}

\subsection*{(b) with $\delta a$: the disturbance is DEFINED by the mismatch}

$\delta a$ is not postulated. It is whatever makes the law-based propagation
exact:

\begin{equation*}
\ba_w[k+1] = \ba_w[k] + \bigl(1-\ba_w[k]\bigr)^{2}\Bigl[
  \Delta\bw_d[k] + (1-\lambda_c)\,\delta\bw_3[k]
  + F_{dw}[k]\,e_{\ba_w}[k] + \varepsilon_w[k]\Bigr] + \delta a[k]
\end{equation*}

Subtracting the true line above gives $\delta a$ in closed form:

\begin{align*}
\delta a[k]
  &= \Bigl[\ba'_{\mathrm{true}}[k] - \bigl(1-\ba_w[k]\bigr)^{2}\Bigr]
     \Bigl[\Delta\bw_d[k] + (1-\lambda_c)\,\delta\bw_3[k]
       + F_{dw}[k]\,e_{\ba_w}[k] + \varepsilon_w[k]\Bigr] \\[0.4em]
  &= \bigl(1-\ba_w[k]\bigr)^{2}
     \left[\frac{1}{b_{\mathrm{state}}(\bw[k])} - 1\right]
     \Bigl[\Delta\bw_d[k] + (1-\lambda_c)\,\delta\bw_3[k]
       + F_{dw}[k]\,e_{\ba_w}[k] + \varepsilon_w[k]\Bigr]
\end{align*}

Three properties follow directly and are used in S8:

\begin{equation*}
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{(i)}   & \delta a[k] \text{ is proportional to the increment: it is ZERO
                during a hold.} \\[0.3em]
\text{(ii)}  & 1/b_{\mathrm{state}} - 1 \in [-0.133,\,-0.072] \text{ is one-signed,
                but } \delta a \text{ is NOT:} \\
             & \quad \mathrm{sign}(\delta a) = -\,\mathrm{sign}(\text{increment}),
                \text{ so it reverses with the direction of travel.} \\
             & \quad \text{Measured on the canonical scenario:}\
                \textstyle\sum_k \delta a = +0.0680 \text{ (descent)},\
                -0.0401 \text{ (ascent)}, \\
             & \quad \text{and EXACTLY ZERO over a closed oscillation cycle ---
                both } \bar{a}'_{\mathrm{true}}\mathrm{d}\bw \text{ and} \\
             & \quad (1-\ba_{\mathrm{true}})^{2}\mathrm{d}\bw
                \text{ are exact differentials in } \bw. \\
             & \quad \textbf{The whole disturbance is acquired on the descent;
                the oscillation offers} \\
             & \quad \textbf{a constant-}\delta a\textbf{ model nothing to lock
                onto.} \\[0.3em]
\text{(iii)} & (1-\ba_w)^{2} \text{ spans } 0.83 \to 0.0019:\ \delta a
                \text{ varies over three decades.}
\end{array}
\end{equation*}

The process model of S4 nevertheless declares

\begin{equation*}
\delta a[k+1] = \delta a[k]
\end{equation*}

This is a modelling CLAIM, not a consequence of the above. S8 prices what the
claim omits.

Measurement ($d$-step delay; $\ba_{wm} = a_{wm}/a_o$). Identical in both

\begin{align*}

Delay back-off: the reading is the gain $d$ steps ago, so walk it back along

\begin{equation*}
\ba_w[k-d] = \ba_w[k] - \bigl(1-\ba_w[k]\bigr)^{2}\,\nabla_d\bw_d[k]
  - d\,\delta a[k]
\end{equation*}

The third term exists only in (b) and is EXACT there: under
$\delta a[k+1] = \delta a[k]$ the disturbance was applied $d$ times over the
delay, so walking the gain back must walk it back too.

\subsection*{The disturbance container $Q_{55}$}

The process model of S4(b) claims $\delta a[k+1] = \delta a[k]$. S3(b) gives
$\delta a[k]$ in closed form, so what the claim omits is computable, not
assumed:

\begin{equation*}
Q_{55}[k] = \mathrm{Var}\Bigl(\delta a[k+1] - \delta a[k]\Bigr)
\end{equation*}

with, writing $\Sigma[k] := \Delta\bw_d[k] + (1-\lambda_c)\delta\bw_3[k]
+ F_{dw}[k]e_{\ba_w}[k] + \varepsilon_w[k]$ for the increment of S3,

\begin{equation*}
\delta a[k] = \bigl(1-\ba_w[k]\bigr)^{2}
  \left[\frac{1}{b_{\mathrm{state}}(\bw[k])} - 1\right]\Sigma[k]
\end{equation*}

Because $\delta a$ is proportional to $\Sigma$, the increment of $\delta a$ is
driven by the motion, not by time: $Q_{55}$ is ZERO during a hold and grows
with commanded speed. A time-invariant $Q_{55}$ would therefore be the wrong
container even if its magnitude were right.

FIRST ARM: $Q_{55} = 0$. This is the claim ``no stochastic driving of
$\delta a$''; it is NOT the claim that $\delta a$ is constant, which S3(b)
already refutes. Its predicted consequence is that $P_{55}$ decreases
monotonically and $\delta\hat{a}$ freezes late in the run. That prediction is
pre-registered here and is to be measured, not assumed.

PRE-REGISTERED CONSEQUENCE. S3(b)(i) says the true $\delta a$ is zero in a
hold, while $F_e(4,5) = 1$ keeps the model injecting $\delta\hat{a}$ every
step. With $Q_{55} = 0$ the covariance cannot reopen to disown it. So the
final hold should show a spurious ramp in the gain error, of order the
descent accumulation ($\sum_k \delta a = +0.068$ over $1.0$ s) scaled by the
hold duration ($\approx 1.3$ s), i.e.\ $\approx +0.09$ in $\ba$ if unopposed
by $y_2$. Its presence, sign and magnitude are to be measured.

OPEN ITEM (not implemented in the first arm). $\delta a$ inherits the
frequency content of $\Sigma$, so on a sinusoidal trajectory it is dominated
by the commanded frequency rather than being constant or white. The internal
model principle then says the constant model $\delta a[k+1] = \delta a[k]$
cannot reject it asymptotically, and the derivable alternative is a two-state
oscillator at the KNOWN commanded frequency. This is recorded so that the
first arm's residual can be read against it.

\qquad

\end{equation*}

must remove it, and its contract is invariance (sweep it by $10\times$; the
converged behaviour must not move).
