\hd{1.\ Architecture ruling (dual-branch)}

Per-axis augmented state $\mathbf x = [\delta x_1,\ \delta x_2,\ \delta x_3,\ a_x]^{\!\top}$; slot 4 carries two candidate dynamics. Both replace the true reads $\adet$, $a'_x$ by the fitted supplier
\[ \hat\adet[k] = a(\hbd[k];\hat\theta), \qquad \hat a'_x[k] = \frac{\partial a}{\partial \bar h}\Big|_{\hat\theta}(\hbd[k]), \qquad \Delta\hat a_x[k] = \hat a'_x[k]\,\Delta h_d[k]. \]

\underline{\textit{Two slot-4 branches}}
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{RW:}\quad\ & a_x[k+1] = a_x[k] + \Delta\hat a_x[k] + \Delta a_{ram}[k],
   & \mathbf F_e(4,4) &= 1 \\[0.4ex]
\text{AR(1):}\quad & a_x[k+1] = \lc\, a_x[k] + (1-\lc)\,\hat\adet[k] + \Delta\hat a_x[k] + a'_x\,\varepsilon[k],
   & \mathbf F_e(4,4) &= \lc
\end{aligned}
\]

The AR(1) predict reads the fitted \emph{level} $\hat\adet$ (reversion anchor) \emph{and} the fitted \emph{slope} $\hat a'_x$ (FF increment); the RW predict reads only the \emph{slope}.

\hd{Structural fact (G1) --- the reversion consumes the curve LEVEL}
Define the supplier \emph{level} error $\ealev[k] = a_{true}(\hbd[k]) - a(\hbd[k];\hat\theta)$. Under AR(1), $\ealev$ enters the slot-4 prediction with weight $(1-\lc)$ per step. Predict/update ($\hat a^-[k{+}1]=\lc\hat a_x[k]+(1-\lc)\hat\adet[k]+\Delta\hat a_x[k]$, then $\hat a_x[k{+}1]=(1-K)\hat a^-[k{+}1]+K\,y_2$) with constant $\ealev$ and $K=$ near-wall DARE gain of the $a_x$ slot gives the steady-state level-bias transfer
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation*}
T \;:=\; \frac{e_{a_x}^{ss}}{\ealev} \;=\; \frac{(1-K)(1-\lc)}{(1-\lc) + K\,\lc}
\;\xrightarrow[\;K\sim 10^{-3}\;]{}\; 0.99\text{--}1.00
\end{empheq}
i.e. a fitted \emph{level} bias passes $\sim\!1{:}1$ into $\hat a_x$ (the estimator cannot out-vote a persistent anchor bias with $K\!\to\!0$). This is the reason the audit's ``slope-only'' scope fails: the $0.4\%$ AR(1) baseline is bought by the \emph{level} anchor, and $\ealev$ is a first-class error input.

\textbf{Branch trade}: AR(1) consumes LEVEL (bias $T\!\approx\!1$ but $0.4\%$ known-$c$ floor); RW consumes only SLOPE (no $\ealev$ path) but carries the documented known-$c$ near-wall ceiling $|\hat a_z-a_z|=9.4\%$ (\texttt{4state\_del\_hd.tex} L274 caption). Both are derived here; measured L2/L3 rungs pick the winner.

\hd{Error dynamics (supplier errors as exogenous inputs)}
With slope error $e_K[k] = a'_{true}(\hbd[k]) - a'(\hbd[k];\hat\theta)$ (so $\Delta a_x^{true}-\Delta\hat a_x = e_K\,\Delta h_d$), the predict-only gain-error recursion for each branch:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{AR(1):}\quad e_{a_x}[k+1] &= \lc\, e_{a_x}[k] + (1-\lc)\,\ealev[k] \\
   &\quad + e_K[k]\,\Delta h_d[k] + a'_x\,\varepsilon[k] \\
\text{RW:}\qquad e_{a_x}[k+1] &= e_{a_x}[k] + e_K[k]\,\Delta h_d[k] + \Delta a_{ram}[k]
\end{aligned}
\]
$a'_x\varepsilon$ (AR(1)) and $\Delta a_{ram}$ (RW) are the base white drivers ($q_4$; \texttt{4state\_del\_hd\_ar1.tex} p.5, \texttt{4state\_del\_hd.tex} L253). The two \emph{new} inputs $\ealev$ (AR(1) only) and $e_K$ (both) are \textbf{deterministic-dominated} --- structure misfit $+$ frozen-$\theta$ error, slowly varying, \emph{not} white. Their carrier assignment (which $Q/R$ entry, bridge, or offline bias table absorbs them) is \emph{not} $Q_{44}$ inflation; it is fixed in \S3.2 (bias/variance split) and \S3.3 (Ruling 1).

\hd{3.\ Error dynamics $+$ carriers}

\hd{3.1\ Exact error inputs (finite-difference of level evals)}
The FF increment is coded as a difference of two level evaluations (\texttt{da\_x\_pred}, L306), not a slope read:
\[ \Delta\hat a_x[k] = a(\hbd[k];\hat\theta) - a(\hbd[k{-}1];\hat\theta). \]
True deterministic increment $\Delta a_x^{det}[k]=a_{true}(\hbd[k])-a_{true}(\hbd[k{-}1])$; the exact slot-4 FF error input is a level-error difference
\[ \Delta\ealev[k] := \ealev(\hbd[k]) - \ealev(\hbd[k{-}1]), \qquad \ealev(\bar h):=a_{true}(\bar h)-a(\bar h;\hat\theta). \]
Taylor at leading order (${}'\!=\!d/dh$, $\ealev'\!=\!e_K$, $\ealev''\!=\!e_K'$):
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation*}
\Delta\ealev[k] = e_K[k]\,\Delta h_d[k] + \tfrac12 e_K'[k]\,\Delta h_d^2[k] + O(\Delta h_d^3),
\qquad e_K[k]=a'_{true}(\hbd[k])-a'(\hbd[k];\hat\theta).
\end{empheq}
Exact recursions (Sec.\,1 form; leading order used below):
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{RW:}\quad\ & e_{a_x}[k+1] = e_{a_x}[k] + \Delta\ealev[k] + \Delta a_{ram}[k] \\
\text{AR(1):}\quad & e_{a_x}[k+1] = \lc\,e_{a_x}[k] + (1-\lc)\,\ealev(\hbd[k]) + \Delta\ealev[k] + a'_x\varepsilon[k]
\end{aligned}
\]
{\small AR(1) carries the level \emph{twice}: $(1-\lc)\ealev$ (reversion anchor, $O(\ealev)$) $+$ $\Delta\ealev$ (FF, $O(e_K\Delta h_d)\!\ll\!\ealev$). RW carries only $\Delta\ealev$; no $(1-\lc)\ealev$ path. This is the branch split of Sec.\,1 --- RW's level bias enters only through the FF slope-difference, AR(1)'s through the anchor.}

\hd{3.2\ Deterministic/stochastic split $+$ carriers}
\[ \ealev(\bar h,k) = \underbrace{b_{lvl}(\bar h)}_{\text{struct.\ misfit}\,+\,\text{frozen-}\theta} + \underbrace{s_{lvl}[k]}_{(\partial a/\partial\theta)^{\!\top}\!\delta\hat\theta}, \qquad e_K(\bar h,k)= b_K(\bar h)+s_K[k] \]
Jacobian map (RLS posterior $P_\theta$, $\delta\hat\theta=\hat\theta-\theta^\ast$, zero-mean):
\[ \mathrm{Var}(s_{lvl}) = (\partial a/\partial\theta)^{\!\top} P_\theta\, (\partial a/\partial\theta), \qquad
   \mathrm{Var}(s_K) = (\partial a'/\partial\theta)^{\!\top} P_\theta\, (\partial a'/\partial\theta). \]
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{%
\small
\textbf{Carrier rule (bias/variance split).}\ \
\emph{Deterministic} $b_{lvl},\,b_K$ (functions of $\bar h$, slowly varying, NOT white): carrier $\in$ \{offline bias table $e_{ss}(\bar h)$ | deterministic bridge (subtract known lag) | gate (trough, $y_2$ off)\}. \textbf{Never $Q$.}\\
\emph{Stochastic} $s_{lvl},\,s_K$ (RLS noise, white at loop rate): carrier $=$ $Q_{44}$ additive via Jacobian --- RW: $Q_{44}\mathrel{+}=\mathrm{Var}(s_K)\,\Delta h_d^2$ (relative form, \S3.6); AR(1): $Q_{44}\mathrel{+}=(1-\lc)^2\mathrm{Var}(s_{lvl})$ (\S3.5).
}}

\hd{3.3\ $Q_{44}$ inflation of the $e_K$ ramp --- rejected}
Slot-4 input $u[k]=e_K[k]\,\Delta h_d[k]$ is a deterministic ramp, correlation time $\tau_c\approx$ quarter-period $=f_s/(4f)=1600/4=400$ steps. Its only white (unpredictable) content is the one-step innovation
\[ \Delta u[k]=u[k]-u[k-1]\approx e_K\,\Delta^2 h_d[k],\quad \Delta^2 h_d=\ddot h_d\,T_s^2=-A_h\omega^2 T_s^2\sin\omega t, \]
so the white-to-signal ratio is set purely by the sampling kinematics
\[ \frac{q_{eq}}{\mathrm{Var}(u)} \approx \Big(\frac{\Delta u}{u}\Big)^{\!2} = (\omega T_s)^2 = \Big(\frac{2\pi}{1600}\Big)^{\!2} = 1.54\times10^{-5}. \]
Against the base driver $Q_{44}=\mathrm{Var}(\Delta a_{ram})$ ($z$, trough) this gives
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation*}
q_{eq}\ \le\ 6.4\times10^{-5}\,Q_{44}\quad(z)
\end{empheq}
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{%
\small \textbf{Ruling 1:}\ $Q_{44}$ inflation is an \textbf{invalid carrier} for $e_K\Delta h_d$ (white content $<10^{-4}$ of $Q_{44}$). The ramp is $\sim$pure low-frequency: its effect is a \emph{bias}, not a variance. Use the ramp-lag table (\S3.4).}}

{\small $6.4\!\times\!10^{-5}$ (audit C1-02 numerical eval) $=(\omega T_s)^2$ kinematic factor $\times\,\mathrm{Var}(u)/Q_{44}|_{trough}$ ($O(4)$); the $(\omega T_s)^2$ scaling is the load-bearing structural result --- agreement, not disagreement.}

\hd{3.4\ Honest carrier: ramp-lag bias}
RW slot-4 error is restored only by the KF gain $K_{44}$ ($\mathbf F_e(4,4)=1$):
\[ e_{a_x}[k+1] = (1-K_{44})\,e_{a_x}[k] + u[k],\qquad u=e_K\Delta h_d=r\,\hat a'_x\Delta h_d,\quad r:=e_K/\hat a'_x, \]
quasi-static ($u$ const):
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation*}
e_{ss}^{RW}(\bar h) = \frac{r\,\hat a'_x(\bar h)\,\Delta h_d}{K_{44}(\bar h)}
\end{empheq}
AR(1) adds the reversion pole $(1-\lc)$: $\ e_{a_x}[k+1]=(1-K_{44})[\lc\,e_{a_x}[k]+u[k]]$, so
\[ e_{ss}^{AR(1)} = \frac{(1-K_{44})\,r\,\hat a'_x\Delta h_d}{(1-\lc)+K_{44}\lc},
   \qquad \frac{e_{ss}^{AR(1)}}{e_{ss}^{RW}}\approx\frac{K_{44}}{1-\lc}\approx 8\times10^{-3}. \]
The reversion suppresses the FF ramp-lag $\sim\!125\times$ vs RW (trough): the slope-error ramp is an \textbf{RW-branch} problem. (AR(1)'s price is instead the LEVEL bias $(1-\lc)\ealev$, \S3.5, transfer $T\!\approx\!1$.)

\underline{\textit{Sinusoid rejection}}\ \ $u$ reverses sign each half-cycle; the leaky integrator $H(z)=1/(z-(1-K_{44}))$ attenuates the quasi-static peak by
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation*}
A_{rej}(\bar h) = K_{44}\,|H(e^{j\omega T_s})| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1+(\omega T_s/K_{44})^2}}
\end{empheq}
evaluated at the bias-peak \emph{flank} (where $|\Delta h_d|$ is max --- not the trough, where $\Delta h_d\!\to\!0$). Flank $K_{44}\!\approx\!5\times10^{-3}$: $A_{rej}\!\approx\!0.80$ (reproduces audit T1 step 4). The trough $K_{44}=2.4\times10^{-3}$ would give $0.52$, but $\Delta h_d\!\approx\!0$ there so it is not the operative point. {\small (See final message for the flag: the audit's $\times0.80$ holds only at the flank $K_{44}$; a naive trough eval gives $0.52$.)}

\textit{Schmidt consider alternative (optional).}\ Carry $\theta$ as a \emph{consider} parameter --- propagate fixed $P_{\theta\theta}$ and cross $P_{x\theta}$ in the covariance, no $\theta$ state, no gain on $\theta$; inflates slot-4 $P$ by $(\partial a/\partial\theta)P_{\theta\theta}(\partial a/\partial\theta)^{\!\top}$. Captures $s_{lvl},s_K$ (stochastic) but not $b_{lvl},b_K$ (still bias table). Cost: a $4\times n_\theta$ cross block per axis per step, no control-path change.

\hd{3.5\ AR(1) level term in $Q_{44}$}
Only the \emph{stochastic} $s_{lvl}$ is $Q$-able; the deterministic $b_{lvl}$ is a bias (transfer $T\!\approx\!1$, G1) that goes to the table:
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation*}
Q_{44}\ \mathrel{+}=\ (1-\lc)^2\,\mathrm{Var}(s_{lvl}) = (1-\lc)^2\,(\partial a/\partial\theta)^{\!\top} P_\theta\,(\partial a/\partial\theta)
\end{empheq}
{\small The audit's ``$Q_{44}\!+\!=\!(1-\lc)^2\mathrm{Var}(\ealev)$'' is read as the \emph{stochastic} component only; $b_{lvl}^2$ is not a variance and must not enter $Q$ --- it surfaces as the $1{:}1$ level bias, the un-$Q$-able cost of the AR(1) anchor.}

}}

\hd{4.\ $y_2$ known-input correction}

\hd{4.1\ Model-exact telescoped level correction}
Base $y_2$ correction is the known telescoped increment sum (\texttt{sum\_da\_ff}, L307--314, consumed L547): $c[k]=\sum_{i=1}^{d}\Delta a_x[k-i] = \adet[k]-\adet[k-d]$. Under $\theta$-fit it becomes the fitted level difference
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation*}
\hat c[k] = a(\hbd[k];\hat\theta) - a(\hbd[k{-}d];\hat\theta)
\end{empheq}
Like the FF (\S3.1) this is a difference of two fitted \emph{level} evals --- no $a'$ read, exact within the fitted model (no Taylor step). Corrected $y_2=a_{xm}+\hat c$ measures $a_x[k]$ with residual
\[ r_2[k] = \underbrace{n_a[k] - \textstyle\sum_{i=1}^{d}\Delta a_{ram}[k-i]}_{\text{base }r_2}\; -\; e_c[k], \]

\hd{7.4\ Warm-up sequence $+$ FF-enable}

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{%
\small \textbf{FF-enable (with hysteresis).}\ \ enable when $(\partial a'/\partial\theta)^2 P_\theta < (r_{target}\,\hat a'_x)^2$; disable only if $>(2 r_{target}\,\hat a'_x)^2$. The factor $2$ prevents chattering under the $46\%$ chi-sq fluctuation (a single-threshold gate would toggle every few steps near the boundary). \textbf{Requirement}: enable \emph{before} $\bar h\!<\!3$ (else the no-FF ramp lag $\sim\!30\%$, C1-12).}}

\textit{Expected enable time}: $\hat a'_x\!\to\!0$ far field makes the RHS tiny during hold, so enable fires on the descent once $\hat a'_x$ (hence RHS) has grown and $P_\theta$ collapsed; order $\sim\!0.3$--$0.7$\,s into the descent ($\bar h\!\sim\!8$--$12$), comfortably before $\bar h\!<\!3$. Flag: L1-measurable (RLS collapse rate $\times$ descend $\phi$ ramp).

\hd{8.\ Independence --- the physical g-loop}
S3--S7 treated $\ealev/e_K$ as exogenous. Cross-session (2026-07-07) proved algorithmic acyclicity does \emph{not} license this: the loop closes through physics. S8 analyzes the full loop, states the conditions for the exogenous treatment, and specifies the verifying tests.

\hd{8.1\ The full loop map}
\textbf{PHYSICAL} leg (uncuttable --- it is the plant): $\ \hat a_x \xrightarrow{\,f_d\,} \delta x \to \hat\sigma^2_{\delta x_r} \to a_{xm}$.
\ \ \textbf{ALGORITHMIC} return (S6.5 severs algebraic shortcuts): $a_{xm}$ feeds two paths back to $\hat a_x$,
\[
\begin{cases}
\text{P1: } y_2 \to \text{EKF slot 4} \to \hat a_x & (\text{fast, per-step}) \\
\text{P2: RLS} \to \hat\theta \to a(\bar h_d;\hat\theta),\,\text{FF},\,Q_{44}/R_{22} \to \hat a_x & (\text{slow})
\end{cases}
\]
The forbidden-inputs list cuts algebraic shortcuts (selfdet-type), but the physical leg cannot be cut by code discipline.

\hd{8.2\ Mean-path (DC) analysis}
Compose stage log-gains at $g{=}1$: $\ d\ln g/d\ln\hat a_x=-1$; $\ d\ln a_{xm}/d\ln g = dm/dg|_1=-0.41$ (C1-08) $\Rightarrow$ forward physics $d\ln a_{xm}/d\ln\hat a_x=+0.41$. With the anchor$\to\hat a_x$ DC transfer (EWMA $a_{m\_det}$: $1$; RLS: $|P_{tilt}|\!\approx\!0.9$) and lumped $\beta$/lag/$T$ attenuation $\eta\!\approx\!0.76$:
\[ S_{DC} = |dm/dg|_1\cdot(\text{anchor DC})\cdot\eta =
\begin{cases} 0.41\cdot 1\cdot 0.76 \approx 0.31 & (\text{EWMA; matches parallel } S\!\approx\!0.31) \\ 0.41\cdot 0.9\cdot 0.76 \approx 0.28 & (\text{RLS, smaller}) \end{cases} \]
$|S_{DC}|<1$ $\Rightarrow$ unique contracting fixed point (stable). The C1-08 amplification $1/(1-S_{DC})$ is the \textbf{mean equilibrium shift}, not instability: $1/(1{-}0.41){=}1.69$ (the $|dm/dg|$ bound), measured $1/(1{-}0.31){=}1.45$ (production $1.38$--$1.52$). The $+12\%$ decomposes as open-loop $1.6$--$3.7\%$ $+$ $\sim\!8$pt loop-equilibrium shift; the linear $1/(1{-}S)$ captures leading order, the remainder is $m(g)$ nonlinearity.

\hd{8.3\ Fluctuation / escape}
DC contraction does \emph{not} preclude noise-triggered escape. The chi-sq stream $\nu$ (rel.\ std $46\%$/step, $\tau_{int}\!\approx\!39$) enters $\hat\sigma^2_{\delta x_r}\!\to\!a_{xm}\!\to$ anchor every step. Through the \emph{nonlinear} $m(g)$, a rare large downward $a_{xm}$ excursion pushes $\hat a_x$, shifting $g$ into a region where the local $|dm/dg|$ momentarily exceeds the contraction margin; the per-step anchor gain $\alpha$ sets how much of each excursion commits to $\hat a_x$. Below the cliff excursions decay; above, a large deviation escapes the finite basin ($S_{DC}\!<\!1$ is only \emph{local}). Seed-dependence reflects the rare trigger: $\alpha^\ast{=}0.008$ ($0.007\!\to\!0/5$, $0.008\!\to\!1/5$, $0.010\!\to\!3/5$, $0.020\!\to\!5/5$). This is metastable escape, not mean instability. The operative fluctuation-safety condition is S6 Ruling 4: $\alpha_{eq}\le\alpha^\ast/M$, $M\!\ge\!4$ (margin sized to clear the seed-dependent onset $0.007$--$0.008$ under the $46\%$ fluctuation; $M{=}1$ would sit at the $1/5$-escape point).

\hd{8.5\ Shared-noise covariance}
$e_K$ (via $s_K$) and $e_{a_x}$ (via $K_{44}$) are both driven by the \emph{same} chi-sq stream $\nu$ (the $a_{xm}$ noise, audit $n_a$): $e_{a_x}$ gets $K_{44}\nu$ (P1), $s_K=(\partial a'/\partial\theta)K_\theta\nu$ (P2). Over the $\tau_{int}$ window,
\[ \mathrm{Cov}(e_K,e_{a_x}) \sim (\partial a'/\partial\theta)\,K_\theta\,K_{44}\,\mathrm{Var}(\nu)\,\tau_{int},\qquad
   |\mathrm{Cov}| \le \rho_\nu\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(s_K)\,\mathrm{Var}(e_{a_x})}. \]
Both anchor gains are small ($K_\theta\!\sim\!(1{-}\lambda_f)\!\sim\!10^{-3}$, $K_{44}\!\sim\!10^{-3}$ near wall), so $\rho_\nu$ is a product of two tiny gains: the cross is second-order. Consequence: treating $e_K,e_{a_x}$ independent in the S3 carrier bookkeeping makes $P$ \emph{optimistic} by $2\,\mathrm{Cov}/Q_{44}\lesssim 5\%$ of $Q_{44}$ (order estimate); identically for $\mathrm{Cov}(s_{lvl},e_{a_x})$. $\ll$ the $1\%$ budget (C1-09).

\hd{8.6\ The conditioned-exogeneity license}
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{%
\small \textbf{Ruling 5 (conditioned exogeneity).} $\ealev/e_K$ may be treated as exogenous in the S3--S7 bookkeeping \textbf{iff} all four hold:
(i) forbidden-inputs list (S6.5) --- \emph{algorithmic} layer;
(ii) anchor-loop gain Ruling 4, $\lambda_f\!\ge\!0.999$ --- \emph{fluctuation} layer;
(iii) shared-noise Cov within the \S8.5 bounds --- \emph{bookkeeping} layer;
(iv) verified by the \S8.7 ablations.
Violation of \emph{any} $\Rightarrow$ the S3 carrier table is invalid (the loop is not exogenous; the $Q/R$ sizing is wrong).}}

\hd{8.7\ Verification spec (interventions, L2)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{(a) freeze-$\theta$ oracle}: same seed $+$ \texttt{clear}; live RLS vs $\theta$ frozen at its converged value. The difference isolates the supplier-loop contribution. \emph{Holds}: difference $\ll$ budget. \emph{Fails}: difference grows/diverges.
\item \textbf{(b) cut-physics}: $a_{ctrl}{:=}$ true gain (oracle) while the estimator runs normally --- kills the physical leg, isolates the measurement chain. \emph{Holds}: $\hat a_x$ error drops to open-loop $1.6$--$3.7\%$ (confirms the $\sim\!8$pt was loop-driven).
\item \textbf{(c) cut-$y_2$}: $y_2$ permanently gated --- isolates P2 (RLS) from P1. \emph{Holds}: RLS-only path stays bounded; \emph{Fails}: escape persists with $y_2$ off (RLS-loop unsafe).
\item \textbf{(d) $\lambda_f\in\{0.99,0.999\}$ cliff-transfer pair}: S6 Ruling 4 predicts $0.99$ escapes (above cliff), $0.999$ safe. Confirms/falsifies the $\alpha_{eq}\!\leftrightarrow\!$cliff mapping.
\end{itemize}

\hd{8.8\ $p_{md}$ future channel}
$p_{md}$ (the LP mean $\delta\bar x_m$) carries $\;p_{md}\approx(1-g)\,D[k]+\text{noise}$, $\ D[k]=p_d[k{+}1]-\lc p_d[k]-(1-\lc)p_d[k{-}d]$ (known trajectory functional, $D{=}0$ at hold). This is a \emph{signed first-moment} $g$-observation, currently discarded. It would de-confound $g$ (scale) from $\theta$ (shape) --- a \emph{direct} $g$ estimate independent of the $a_{xm}$ scale, breaking the confound that Ruling 5 must otherwise bound. Out of MVP: needs its own noise/bias analysis ($D$-SNR, the $(1-g)$ linearization, control-loop interaction). Forward-ref T3.
