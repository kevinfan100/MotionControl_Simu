STAGE A PRE-REGISTRATION -- ap_known joint arm (2026-08-24, Mac, written BEFORE the run)
=========================================================================================

QUESTION
  a_hat is biased +18~20% near the wall on the deep band. 87% of that is
  unattributed (fdet accounts for ~13%, measured 08-24). Two families remain:

    [H-law]   the gain law's slope is wrong. a_bar' = b(1-a_bar)^2 with a
              CONSTANT b cannot serve both exact anchors (8/9 far field,
              1 at contact), and at the deep trough a_bar is the difference
              of two numbers ~19 that nearly cancel.
    [H-cov]   row-4 covariance mechanics: P(4,1) carries a systematic offset
              and F_e(4,3) grows 317x near the wall as its conduit; the y1
              leg is 86% rectification (Cov(K1,innov1)) in the descent.

  SEPARATOR: feed the TRUE slope in exogenously (opts.ap_known) and see
  whether the bias survives.  COST: ~12 min for both arms, 100 seeds each.

WHAT THE ARM ACTUALLY CHANGES  (read from code, NOT from the option name)
  controller:1080-1083 and local_build_F_e_formC (1500-1530). Turning on
  ap_known changes THREE things at once, so this is registered as a JOINT
  test, not a clean single-factor test:
    (i)   predict's integrand becomes the true slope        <- the intended one
    (ii)  F_e(4,3) = (1-lc)*a_bar_p is rebuilt from the true slope
    (iii) A_a = 0, so F_e(4,4)'s A_a*M term is DELETED
  A fourth item -- dap_db_i frozen at controller:1070 BEFORE the 1081
  override, leaving F_e(4,5)/H(2,5) on the law's slope -- is NEUTRALISED by
  running both arms with b LOCKED (arm 'bmid', lock_b = true, b = 8/9), which
  makes slot 5 provably inert (zero P0, zero Jacobian, zero K).

  Therefore the readable fork is:
    bias SURVIVES  -> the whole {slope, F_e(4,3) aperture, A_a*M} family is
                      excluded in one run. Cheap and decisive. Next target
                      becomes the y1 rectification / P(4,1) offset itself.
    bias VANISHES  -> attribution requires a further split; NO single member
                      of the family may be named from this run alone.

  COUPLINGS THAT SURVIVE (why a_hat is readable on this arm, unlike the
  08-21 true-a arm):
    F_e(3,4) = -F_dw is UNCHANGED and the coupling it models is still
    physically present, because the plant is untouched -- only the filter's
    slope integrand is replaced. On the true-a arm the plant coupling was
    removed while the filter still modelled it, which collapsed a_hat and
    made every a_hat number on that arm unreadable.
    a_bar_p*F_dw inside F_e(4,4) is RE-EVALUATED at the true slope, not
    deleted.

WIRING SANITY CHECKS -- LOOK AT THESE FIRST, BEFORE ANY BIAS NUMBER
  (project rule 13: write down what would give a broken harness away, and
   read that before the RMS)
  W1. b_hat_out must be EXACTLY 8/9 = 0.888889 at every step on BOTH arms.
      If it moves, lock_b is not wired and the pair is void.
  W2. On the ap_known arm, a_prime_out must equal a_prime_true_out evaluated
      at the PREVIOUS step's true height (driver:1128 uses hb_prev). Expect a
      one-step lag, not equality at the same index. If a_prime_out instead
      tracks the law's b(1-a_hat)^2, ap_known never reached the controller
      and the arm is void.  (This is the exact failure mode of the dead
      law_b_formC knob found today: a flag that is read and then never used.)
  W3. The two arms must be PAIRED: same seeds 1:100, same scenario 'deep',
      identical otherwise. Seed-0 sanity: the two arms must differ from step 1
      onwards but their a_true_out must be IDENTICAL (the plant is untouched).
      If a_true_out differs, the arm changed the plant and the test is void.
  W4. Row 1 of every logged field is the controller's init-only call (all
      zeros). It must be dropped before any statistic. Signature if missed:
      an exact all-zero row.

PREDICTIONS (registered before the run; thresholds fixed here)
  Primary readout: near-wall a_hat bias, defined as
      bias = mean over the trough hold (t >= 3.75 s) of
             (a_bar_hat - a_true/a_nom) / (a_true/a_nom)
  measured on z, paired per seed against the 'bmid' baseline.

  Baseline expectation (NOT yet measured on 'bmid'; the +18~20% figure was
  measured on 'best'):  the 'bmid' baseline bias is expected to land within
  +/-3 pp of the 'best' baseline, because b is locked at the same 8/9 that
  'best' seeds from and b_hat was measured to move only 26% of b_true.
  If 'bmid' baseline differs from 'best' by more than 3 pp, the b free-run is
  NOT negligible and that fact is itself the headline -- report it and do not
  bury it.

  P1 [H-law dominant]   ap_known bias <= 1/3 of baseline bias (i.e. >=67%
                        of the bias removed), paired t significant.
  P2 [H-cov dominant]   ap_known bias >= 2/3 of baseline bias (i.e. <=33%
                        removed).
  P3 [mixed]            anything between. Then NEITHER family is excluded and
                        the honest report is "joint arm removed X%, no
                        member attributable".
  P4 [instrument]       F_e(4,3) is rebuilt from the true slope, so its
                        near-wall growth must CHANGE on the ap_known arm.
                        a_prime_out at the trough should move toward the true
                        slope. If a_prime_out is unchanged between arms, W2
                        has failed regardless of what the bias did.
  P5 [y1 leg]           If the bias survives (P2), the y1 leg decomposition
                        (E[K1]*E[innov1] vs Cov(K1,innov1)) should be
                        essentially UNCHANGED between arms at the trough hold
                        (baseline: total +0.01517, systematic +0.01507,
                        covariance +0.00010). A large change in the leg split
                        with an unchanged bias would mean two mechanisms are
                        trading off and the single-number readout is hiding it.

WHAT THIS RUN CANNOT ANSWER
  - It cannot name which of {slope, aperture, A_a*M} is responsible if the
    bias vanishes. That needs Stage B (two default-off diagnostic flags in the
    controller), which is a production change and is NOT part of this run.
  - It says nothing about x/y (deferred by the user) and nothing about the
    far-field IF_eff defect (a separate, <1%-impact item).
  - 'bmid' is not the production arm ('best' is). Any number here is a
    statement about the 4-state writing of the tex, not about production.
