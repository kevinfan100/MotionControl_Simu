## 2. `test/meng-ch4` (`~/Code/MotionControl_Simu-meng-ch4`)

**Purpose.** Reproduce Meng's PhD thesis chapter 4 (= the TIE 2025 journal paper) inside our simulation:
his scenarios A and B, his three acceptance criteria, and â tracking the true gain.
Then compare his "estimate the value" estimator with our "estimate the function" estimator on the same scenario.

**State.** Closed on 2026-08-17/18 ("Meng 線關帳", ledger §43–§46).
- Scenario A and Scenario B reproduced; Scenario B meets all five criteria (§38).
- The best Meng-style configuration is `run_meng_ch4_best.m`, the "champion runner". It adds:
  - stale-consumption feedforward;
  - per-axis y₂ whitening;
  - a height-scheduled forgetting factor;
  - empirical C recalibration.
- Paired comparison (conclusion 8, §46), same canonical scenario, z-gain relative error, 4 seeds. Our function estimator
  (formB_ws, production) against the best Meng configuration:

  | Metric | formB_ws | Best Meng configuration | Ratio |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Oscillation band | 0.27–0.47 % | 9.2–11.6 % | ~25× |
  | Hold drift | ≤ 0.45 %/s | 2.6–7.5 %/s | ~6–100× |

  Caveat: each side used its own operating point, λc 0.7 vs 0.4.
- Open items, recorded as limitations:
  - x overshoot in Scenario B (1.28–1.31), with five single-variable eliminations on record;
  - z bias +5 to +8 %;
  - the scheduled-λ_f arm is unusable on Scenario B until a J-accounting fix lands (§46 note).

**Evidence.** Simulation only.

**Authoritative files** (on the branch):
- `reference/eq17_analysis/meng_ch4_spec_ledger.md`: the single source of truth, §1–§46, with every number and run named.
  §1–§3 classify the paper's specs as stated / absent / admitted-wrong.

  `test_script/scratch/plot_meng_ch4_{s0,sA,sAfinal,sB_pages,gain_cmp,...}.m`; the best configuration is `run_meng_ch4_best.m`.

  **[unverified]** Where the generated PNGs sit on disk; probably `test_results/` in that worktree (gitignored).

**Departures from Meng's derivation.** I can confirm two, both from the ledger:
1. **The two-step â delay.** Meng's journal notes under Eq. (21) that â carries an uncorrected two-step delay
   (e_ax ≈ a_x[k−2] − â_x[k]). We correct it in the measurement matrix: H(2,7) = −d instead of 0 (ledger §3, §4).
2. **The stale-consumption known input.** His control law (4.4) consumes the previous posterior. So the true slot-3 dynamics
   carry a known input u₃[k] = (1−λc)·Δx̂₃ − Δx̂₄, which his filter treats as noise. We add it to the predict step
   (flag `ch4_stale_ff`). The innovation honesty ratio goes 2.52 → 1.00 (ledger §25), and the LOO checks keep it (§36, §44).

**[unverified]** OUTLINE says "兩處對原論文推導的修正". I believe these are the two, but nothing in the repo labels them that way;
the author should confirm. Other changes in the ledger are additions, not corrections of his derivation:
- per-axis y₂ whitening;
- the height-scheduled λ_f;
- the empirical C recalibration;
- the third F_e row, which follows our control law (3.6), not his (4.11). That is a different control law, not a fix.

## 3. `test/motion-test` (`~/Code/MotionControl_Simu-motion-test`, this worktree)

**Purpose.** The research mainline: estimate the position-dependent motion gain ā when the wall-correction function c(h̄)
is unknown, by estimating the constants of a gain law ("estimate the function, not the value"), with the estimator
started from the plane-wall law and the wall type given only to the plant.
Two lines run side by side:
- **Layer 1:** a 5-state estimator that does not use the slope ā′, as the baseline.
- **Slope-law line:** ā′ = (1−c)/ĥ² with one constant c ("c-only").

**State** (latest commit 2026-10-04, `7d715b4`; much later work is **uncommitted**, see below).
- Works:
  - The c-only slope law with process noise q₅₅ = P_fix²·I[k], which holds the variance of c at P_fix.
  - The shared test trajectory `ramp_tri`.
  - The Layer-1 5-state estimator with a fixed ratio Q44 = ε·R₂ (ε = 1e-5).
  - Production defaults pass their acceptance script (`test_script/integration/verify_formC_b_production_defaults.m`, ALL PASS 2026-10-06).
- Main finding (2026-10-05/06, ledger O52, R79):
  - c_eff = 1 − h²ā′_true depends on height (cell wall 0.56–0.74 within 1.1–2 R), so a constant c can only follow it with lag.
  - Q settings (single P_fix, a height split, Q from c's drift) trade lag against noise. None removes the near-wall error.
  - With a wide, fast oscillation (1.1–10 R at 2.5 Hz, same speed as the descent), ĉ does follow c_eff (53 % of its swing
    at √P_fix 0.15, 84 % at 0.35). The remaining error is explained by the memory length 1/P_fix.
- Unfinished:
  - The two-constant slope law F2, ā′ = (1−c)/(h+δ)², whose constants are constant for the plane and cell walls.
    Implemented (flag `plaw_dc_slope`, default off) but not yet run on `ramp_tri`; it is the next step.
  - Sphere wall: needs a shape constant (open item O30).
  - The thermal-term symbol clash ε in the Layer-1 document.
- History, closed:
  - powerlaw / expgain / Form B / Form C b-state / [1/1] Padé / δ-law / one-constant slope-law variants.
  - Each is closed in `reference/eq17_analysis/hypotheses_formC_b.md` and `agent_docs/eq17/mainline-history.md`.
